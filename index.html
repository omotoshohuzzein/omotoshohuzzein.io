Plain-Language Guide
What does this work actually mean for you?
Not a finance expert? No problem. Here's what each core service does in plain terms — and why it matters to your business.
Budgeting
Think of a budget as your business's financial game plan for the year.
  • A top-down budget starts with the big picture — leadership sets the total spend, then it flows down to departments.
  • A bottom-up budget starts from each team's real needs and builds up to the full company number.
  • I track how your actual spending compares to the plan every month — so you catch overspending before it becomes a problem.
  • I build the templates, run the reports, and flag the variances — so your management team can focus on decisions, not spreadsheets.
Top-downBottom-upVariance tracking
Metrics, KPIs & Modeling
KPIs are the vital signs of your business — I make sure you're reading them correctly.
  • I identify the numbers that matter most for your business — revenue growth, margins, customer metrics, and more.
  • I build financial models that let you ask "what if?" — what happens if sales drop 20%? If costs rise? If you hire 10 more staff?
  • I translate complex financial data into clear dashboards your leadership team can actually use and understand.
  • Every metric is linked back to a decision — so reporting is never just a box-ticking exercise.
KPI dashboardsScenario modelingRatio analysis
Due Diligence
Before you invest in or buy a business, you need to know exactly what you're getting.
  • I review the target company's historical financials to verify the numbers are real and accurately presented.
  • I stress-test the assumptions behind revenue forecasts and cost projections — so you don't pay for growth that will never materialise.
  • I identify hidden risks: off-balance-sheet liabilities, overstated assets, unusual cash flow patterns.
  • You get a clear, jargon-free findings report with a summary of risks and a recommendation on how to proceed.
Risk identificationAssumption reviewFindings report
Audit & Assurance
Audit is about giving you — and your stakeholders — confidence that the numbers are right.
  • If you've inherited a financial model from someone else, I check it thoroughly for errors, broken formulas, and flawed logic.
  • I review your internal financial controls — the processes that prevent mistakes and fraud from going undetected.
  • I ensure your financial statements comply with IFRS or local accounting standards, so you're prepared for external auditors.
  • The result is a written assurance report that you can share with investors, boards, or lenders as evidence of financial integrity.
Model auditIFRS complianceControls review
